# NSCalendarDate basics

Build nscalendardate-basic.m against Foundation/CoreFoundation and run it under Darling. It checks the NSDate subclass NSCalendarDate against the public documentation (developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/nscalendardate and the pages of its members, deprecated since macOS 10.10, and the NSDate subclassing notes): `+calendarDate`, `+dateWithYear:month:day:hour:minute:second:timeZone:` and `-initWithYear:...timeZone:` with GMT and fixed-offset time zones, the nine date element accessors on known dates (the reference date 2001-01-01, 2000-01-01 a Saturday, 2024-07-19 a Friday), the instant as `timeIntervalSinceReferenceDate`, `setTimeZone:` changing the elements and not the instant, `calendarFormat` and `setCalendarFormat:`, `-dateByAddingYears:months:days:hours:minutes:seconds:` with carries into the next unit and the leap day, comparison with plain NSDate objects (`isEqual:`, `compare:`, `timeIntervalSinceDate:`, `hash`), `copy`, `+distantFuture` and `+distantPast` giving NSCalendarDate objects (called before the NSDate versions, which cache one shared object), and an application subclass with an instance variable of its own.

Baseline: against the base commit the header `NSCalendarDate.h` only holds a comment, the class has no factory or accessor declarations (the compiler reports the methods as not found), and the first call raises `unrecognized selector`; candidate: zero failures.

The pages for the members give only declarations. Everything that they do not state is marked GUESS in the source: the default calendar format `%Y-%m-%d %H:%M:%S %z`, a nil time zone meaning the default time zone, `dayOfWeek` counting Sunday as 0, `dayOfYear` starting at 1, the epoch of `dayOfCommonEra`, the copying of the format, the time zone and format of the result of `-dateByAddingYears:...`, January 31 plus one month, and the subclass being kept by the factory, `copy` and the addition. Daylight saving transitions are not tested. Formatting and parsing with the calendar format (`description`, `descriptionWithCalendarFormat:`, `dateWithString:calendarFormat:` and friends) are not implemented and not tested.
